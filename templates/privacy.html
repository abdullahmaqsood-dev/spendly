Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, log expenses, or contact us. This may include:

  • Personal information (name, email address, etc.)
  • Financial transaction data (amounts, categories, dates, descriptions)
  • Usage data (how you interact with the app, features used)
  • Device and technical information (IP address, browser type, etc.)

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our service
  • Process and categorize your financial transactions
  • Send you important notifications about your account
  • Provide personalized insights and recommendations
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our products and services based on usage patterns

Data Storage

We store your data securely using industry-standard security measures. Your financial data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our service.

Third Party Services

We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business and provide our service, subject to strict confidentiality obligations. These may include:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Payment processors (if applicable)
  • Analytics service providers
  • Customer support platforms

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us.
